The warehouse: what the team builds and how it gets used

The warehouse for this project, from staged source data to the marts the owner, the reorder list and the forecast model read. It covers every dimension, fact table and mart, the conventions every table follows, and how a nightly load runs.

1 · What it is and why the project needs it

The warehouse is the Trusted and Refined zones of our data lake (data-flow.html): one star schema of conformed dimensions and fact tables, plus one mart per business question. It is the only place business rules live. Dashboards, the reorder list and the forecast model read from it and never from a source system.

The reason is easiest to see with one question: what was net revenue by channel on Friday?

Asked of the sources: 30 lines

The query has to carry six rules itself: union two shapes, UTC → Bangkok, business day 10:00–02:00, MAX not SUM on Grab's repeated commission, parse modifier prices out of text, map QR → QR_DINEIN. The POS has also already overwritten last week's prices.

Asked of the warehouse: 7 lines

SELECT channel_code,
       SUM(net_revenue_thb)
FROM dw.fct_order
JOIN dw.dim_channel USING (channel_sk)
JOIN dw.dim_date USING (date_sk)
WHERE calendar_date = '2026-09-04'
GROUP BY channel_code

Same answer (Grab 599.9, phone 408.0, QR 808.0 THB on the prototype data). The rules were applied once, at load time, so no report can get them wrong.

6 · Conventions every table follows

These are the rules the team applies to every new table. The figures come from the prototype in notebooks/warehouse_design.ipynb, where each rule is broken on purpose to show what it protects.

ConventionWhat it protects (prototype figure)
Grain stated firstEvery table has a one-sentence grain. Order money never sits on line rows: joining and summing would put Grab's Friday commission at 403.5 instead of 257.1.
Policy is snapshottedPrice, cost and OT multiplier are copied onto the fact when they happen. Otherwise Saturday's price rise rewrites Friday's Pepperoni L revenue from 1,037 to 1,047.
Lists become tablesNo list in a column. Modifiers as rows put Friday's mozzarella variance at −20 g; as text it would read −100 g.
Store what adds upFacts hold additive parts; ratios are computed when queried. Levels like stock counts are never summed over time (16.3 kg "on hand" vs 5.2 kg real).
One FK per sourceNo shared reference column. A polymorphic key joined a 2 kg cheese delivery to a pizza with no error.
Surrogate keys, SCD2Facts hold warehouse keys only. Menu items, recipes and staff keep every version; facts join to the version valid on their business day.
Business day oncedate_sk is assigned at load (10:00–02:00). No query downstream does time arithmetic.

7 · How a nightly load works

Quality gates that stop the load (full list in README §10):

  • A business key with no dimension match (e.g. a new Grab item not in BRG_CHANNEL_MENU_ITEM) goes to quarantine, with an alert. Revenue is never silently dropped.
  • Line-level discounts must sum to the order-level discount for every order.
  • At most one source FK per inventory movement, matching movement_type.
  • Upsert on (channel_sk, external_order_id), so a re-downloaded export cannot double revenue.
  • The last 3 business days are reprocessed every night to catch late-arriving orders.
