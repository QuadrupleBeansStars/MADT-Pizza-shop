The warehouse: what the team builds and how it gets used

The warehouse for this project, from staged source data to the aggregate tables the owner, the reorder list and the forecast model read. It covers every dimension, fact table and data mart, the dimensional conventions every table follows (MADT8102 chapter 5), and how a nightly load runs.

1 · What it is and why the project needs it

The warehouse is the Trusted and Refined zones of our data lake (data-flow.html). Of the three architectures in chapter 5 it is a Kimball dimensional data warehouse: one integrated set of star schemas on conformed dimensions, at the lowest grain we receive, which analysts may query directly. A data mart is a subject area inside it (Sales, Delivery, Customer, Inventory and purchasing, Labour), each served by aggregate tables. It is the only place business rules live. Dashboards, the reorder list and the forecast model read from it and never from a source system.

The reason is easiest to see with one question: what was net revenue by channel on Friday?

Asked of the sources: 30 lines

The query has to carry six rules itself: union two shapes, UTC → Bangkok, business day 10:00–02:00, MAX not SUM on Grab's repeated commission, parse modifier prices out of text, map QR → QR_DINEIN. The POS has also already overwritten last week's prices.

Asked of the warehouse: 7 lines

SELECT channel_code,
       SUM(net_revenue_thb)
FROM dw.fct_order
JOIN dw.dim_channel USING (channel_key)
JOIN dw.dim_date USING (date_key)
WHERE full_date = '2026-09-04'
GROUP BY channel_code

Same answer (Grab 599.9, phone 408.0, QR 808.0 THB on the prototype data). The rules were applied once, at load time, so no report can get them wrong.

Access rule
Dashboards and tools read the aggregate tables. Analysts may query the dw stars directly, which the Kimball architecture explicitly allows. Nothing outside the pipeline reads raw or stg. When a new question gets asked every week, it becomes an aggregate table.

3 · Dimensions

Every dimension has a _key surrogate key, a _nk natural key, flags stored as readable values ("Weekend" / "Weekday", not true / false), codes stored with their descriptions, and three special-case rows: 0 = Not applicable, 1 = Unknown, 2 = Invalid. A fact never holds a NULL key.

Every dimension is built once and shared, at every level of its hierarchy. That is what lets sales be compared with stock (through menu item and recipe) and with labour (through date) by drilling across, so the reorder loop and labour-vs-sales are routine queries rather than projects. Without it, the warehouse becomes islands of information.

6 · Conventions every table follows

These are the chapter 5 rules the team applies to every new table. The figures come from the prototype in notebooks/warehouse_design.ipynb, where each rule is broken on purpose to show what it protects.

ConventionWhat it protects (prototype figure)
Grain first, drill acrossEvery fact has a one-sentence grain, and facts with different grain are different tables. Two facts are compared by drilling across, never joined: joining orders to lines puts Grab's Friday commission at 403.5 instead of 257.1.
No NULL keysSpecial-case rows instead. With a NULL customer_key, 2,327 of 3,782 THB of revenue silently drops out of a report by membership; with row 0 it is reported as Not applicable.
Late data is loadedAn unmapped item is loaded against row 1, Unknown, with an alert, and re-pointed later. Holding it back would understate sales.
Facts point at dimensionsEach fact carries a key to every dimension it is analysed by, never a key to another fact. Lineage uses a typed degenerate reference, checked by the ETL.
Policy is snapshottedPrice, cost and OT multiplier are copied onto the fact when they happen. Otherwise Saturday's price rise rewrites Friday's Pepperoni L revenue from 1,037 to 1,047.
Lists become tablesNo list in a column. Modifiers as rows put Friday's mozzarella variance at −20 g; as text it would read −100 g.
Store what adds upNonadditive measures are broken into additive parts. Levels like stock counts are never summed over time (16.3 kg "on hand" vs 5.2 kg real).
Junk and degenerateLow-cardinality codes and flags go to junk dimensions; only transaction identifiers stay on the fact as degenerate dimensions.
No snowflakesRelated attributes are stored together and kept redundant; integrity is enforced by the ETL. Role sits on DIM_STAFF.
Business day oncedate_key is assigned at load (10:00–02:00). No query downstream does time arithmetic.

7 · How a nightly load works

Quality checks on every load (full list in README §10):

  • No fact column may be NULL. A business key with no dimension match (e.g. a new Grab item not in BRG_CHANNEL_MENU_ITEM) is loaded against row 1, Unknown, with an alert, and re-pointed once mapped. Revenue is never dropped or understated.
  • Line-level discounts must sum to the order-level discount for every order, checked by drilling across on order_number. This one fails the batch.
  • Every inventory movement's source document exists in the fact its type names, and the sign of qty_delta matches its direction.
  • Upsert on (channel_key, order_number), so a re-downloaded export cannot double revenue.
  • The last 3 business days are reprocessed every night to catch late-arriving orders.
