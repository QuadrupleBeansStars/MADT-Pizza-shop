The problem

Lower waste, higher sales

Six systems, no single answer

QR, phone, Grab, LINE MAN, ShopeeFood and Robinhood each keep their own records, names and time zones.

Commission hides in the gross

Delivery apps take up to 30%. "Sales" on the platform screen is not what the shop keeps.

Waste is a guess

Stock is ordered by feel. Extra cheese is used but never counted, so it looks like shrinkage.

Higher sales

Know net revenue by channel, the items that sell, and which add-ons earn.

Lower waste

Know what was actually used versus counted, then order stock to the forecast.

Speaker: the two business goals frame every design decision after this.

Design · what we store

One sales star, at line grain

FCT_ORDER_LINE

One row per menu item per order. The order is the header, the line is the body; commission and delivery fee are allocated to lines.

FCT_ORDER_LINE_OPTION

One row per option picked from the POS list: extra cheese, no chili, thin crust. No free text.

Date

business day 10:00–02:00

Channel

own vs aggregator

Customer

members only, else row 0

Menu item

SCD2 price history

Option

the pick list

Order info

status, cancelled
