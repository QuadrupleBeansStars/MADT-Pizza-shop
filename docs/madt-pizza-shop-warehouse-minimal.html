Minimal warehouse: three facts, three aggregates, one dashboard

The scope agreed with the teacher. The sales star from section 4 of the full model, built end to end so the owner can see revenue by channel, what sells, and what add-ons earn. Every number below comes from notebooks/warehouse_minimal.ipynb.

1 · Scope

In

  • Two sales sources: the POS (QR, phone) and the delivery-app exports
  • Six conformed dimensions and one bridge
  • Three fact tables: FCT_ORDER, FCT_ORDER_LINE, FCT_ORDER_LINE_MODIFIER
  • Three aggregate tables, one per fact
  • One consumer: the owner dashboard

Out, for now

  • Stock and reorder, purchasing
  • Staff shifts and labour cost
  • Payments and promotions
  • Delivery-time events
  • Forecast model and customer segmentation

The design rules do not change with the scope. This is still a Kimball dimensional warehouse following MADT8102 chapter 5: _key surrogate keys, special-case rows (0 Not applicable, 1 Unknown, 2 Invalid) instead of NULL keys, a junk dimension for order status, degenerate order and line numbers, and facts compared by drilling across.

5 · Aggregate tables

Aggregate tableGrain: one row =Built fromColumns
mart_channel_dailybusiness date × channelFCT_ORDERorders, gross_sales_thb, commission_thb, net_revenue_thb, day name, weekend flag, channel type and ownership
mart_sales_dailybusiness date × channel × SKUFCT_ORDER_LINEunits_sold, orders, item_sales_thb, item name, category, size
mart_modifier_dailybusiness date × channel × modifierFCT_ORDER_LINE_MODIFIERtimes_applied, modifier_revenue_thb, modifier type, item category

Each is built only from the warehouse, excludes cancelled orders, and stores additive measures only. Ratios such as average order value are computed by the dashboard from the parts, so they stay correct by day, week or month.
